<p><em>Rob Miller, Branch Manager at MadCity Home Loans, explains VA entitlement — one of the most misunderstood benefits available to Wisconsin veterans.</em></p>

<h3>How does VA loan entitlement work in Wisconsin?</h3>

<p>
  VA loan entitlement is the amount the Department of Veterans Affairs guarantees to the lender if you default. You never see this money — it's a backstop for the lender that's what allows you to buy with zero down and no PMI.
</p>

<h3>Full Entitlement</h3>
<p>
  If you've never used your VA benefit before, or you've paid off a previous VA loan and had entitlement restored, you have <strong>full entitlement</strong>. With full entitlement and a lender like MadCity Home Loans, there is <strong>no loan limit</strong> — you can buy any price home with zero down (as long as you qualify based on income and credit).
</p>

<h3>Reduced (Remaining) Entitlement</h3>
<p>
  If you currently have an active VA loan or sold a home and didn't fully restore your entitlement, you have reduced entitlement. You can still buy another home with VA — but you may need a down payment to cover the gap between your remaining entitlement and the county loan limit.
</p>

<h3>Bonus (Tier 2) Entitlement</h3>
<p>
  Veterans with remaining entitlement can often still buy with zero down using "bonus entitlement" — a second tier of coverage that kicks in for higher-priced homes. We calculate this precisely for every veteran client.
</p>

<h3>How to Restore Full Entitlement</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Sell the home and pay off the VA loan</li>
  <li>Have another veteran assume the loan and substitute their entitlement</li>
  <li>Pay off the loan even if you still own the property (one-time restoration)</li>
</ul>
