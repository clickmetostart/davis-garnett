<h3>What is a DSCR loan and can I use one for a Wisconsin rental property?</h3>

<p>
  A DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio) loan qualifies you based on the <strong>rental income of the property itself</strong> — not your personal income. It's specifically designed for real estate investors and self-employed buyers who have strong rental cash flow but complex personal tax returns.
</p>

<h3>How the DSCR Ratio Works</h3>
<p>
  DSCR = Monthly Rental Income ÷ Monthly Loan Payment (PITI)
</p>
<p>
  Most lenders want a DSCR of <strong>1.0 or higher</strong> — meaning the property generates at least as much rent as it costs to carry. A 1.25 DSCR is even better and often unlocks lower rates.
</p>
<p>
  Example: A Madison duplex renting for $3,200/month with a PITI of $2,600/month has a DSCR of 1.23 — strong enough to qualify.
</p>

<h3>Key Features of DSCR Loans</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>No personal income verification:</strong> No W-2s, no tax returns, no employment history required</li>
  <li><strong>Down payment:</strong> Typically 20–25% down</li>
  <li><strong>Credit score:</strong> Usually 680+ minimum</li>
  <li><strong>Rates:</strong> Slightly higher than conventional — typically 0.5–1.0% above primary residence rates</li>
  <li><strong>Property types:</strong> 1–4 unit rentals, short-term rentals (Airbnb/VRBO), and in some cases 5–8 unit properties</li>
  <li><strong>Short-term rental income:</strong> Some lenders allow us to use Airbnb or VRBO market rent estimates</li>
</ul>

<h3>Who This Is Built For</h3>
<p>
  Self-employed investors who write off most of their income on taxes. Out-of-state investors buying in the Madison market. Anyone building a rental portfolio who doesn't want their personal DTI limiting how many properties they can own.
</p>
