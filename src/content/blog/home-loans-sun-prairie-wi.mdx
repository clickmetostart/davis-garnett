<p>Sun Prairie home values have appreciated consistently alongside the broader Dane County market. <h2>Loan Programs Available to Sun Prairie Buyers</h2>

<h3>FHA Loans</h3>

<p>FHA is one of the most common programs for Sun Prairie's first-time buyer population. With a minimum down payment of 3.5% and flexible credit requirements — qualifying scores can start in the mid-600s — FHA opens the door for buyers who haven't had time to build a large down payment. The FHA loan limit for Dane County is currently above $540,000, which covers the majority of Sun Prairie's market.</p>

<h3>Conventional Loans</h3>

<p>For buyers with 5–20% down and credit scores in the good-to-excellent range, conventional financing typically offers the lowest total cost of ownership. Private mortgage insurance drops off once you hit 20% equity — either at closing or through appreciation and payments over time — making conventional loans the most efficient path for buyers who qualify.</p>

<h3>VA Loans</h3>

<p>Sun Prairie has a meaningful veteran and active-duty military population. <a href="/knowledge-hub/va-benefits-and-options">VA loans</a> — available to eligible veterans, service members, and surviving spouses — offer zero-down financing with no private mortgage insurance requirement. Rob Miller's team handles VA loans regularly and understands the specific steps involved in making a VA purchase work smoothly.</p>

<h3>WHEDA Loans</h3>

<p>Wisconsin's <a href="/knowledge-hub/wheda-loans-wisconsin-down-payment-assistance">WHEDA</a> loan program provides below-market rates and <a href="/knowledge-hub/down-payment-assistance-madison-dane-county-wi">down payment assistance</a> to qualifying buyers. Income limits apply based on household size and county, but many Sun Prairie buyers fall within the eligibility window. WHEDA can be combined with other assistance sources to significantly reduce upfront costs.</p>

<h3>USDA Loans</h3>

<p>Parts of the Sun Prairie area — particularly on the outer edges and in adjacent townships — may qualify for USDA rural financing. USDA offers zero-down purchase loans to income-qualifying buyers in eligible areas. It's worth checking eligibility early in the process for buyers focused on properties east of Sun Prairie proper.</p>

<h3>Non-QM / Non-Conventional Loans</h3>

<p>Sun Prairie attracts independent business owners and self-employed professionals who don't produce standard W-2 income. Bank statement loans and other non-QM products let these buyers document income through 12 or 24 months of bank statements instead of traditional tax returns — opening access to purchase financing that the standard underwriting box would otherwise deny.</p>

<h2>First-Time Buyers in Sun Prairie: Stacking Your Programs</h2>

<p>One of the most underused strategies in Sun Prairie is combining multiple assistance programs at once. A buyer who qualifies for a WHEDA first mortgage might also qualify for:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>WHEDA Easy Close DPA</strong> — down payment and closing cost assistance structured as a second mortgage at below-market rates</li>
  <li><strong>City of Madison HBAD</strong> — if purchasing within Madison city limits, up to approximately $35,000 in forgivable down payment assistance</li>
  <li><strong>Downpayment Plus</strong> — a Federal Home Loan Bank program available through participating lenders that provides up to $10,000 in forgivable grant assistance</li>
</ul>

<p>Sun Prairie buyers who are close to Madison's boundaries should confirm property eligibility before assuming a program applies or doesn't. Rob's team walks through this mapping early in the process to make sure nothing is left on the table.</p>
