<h3>Can I buy a multifamily property with an FHA loan in Wisconsin?</h3>

<p>
  Yes — and this is one of the most powerful wealth-building moves available in Wisconsin right now. FHA allows you to purchase a 1–4 unit property with just <strong>3.5% down</strong>, as long as you occupy one of the units as your primary residence.
</p>

<h3>How It Works (House Hacking)</h3>
<p>
  You buy a duplex, triplex, or fourplex. You live in one unit. You rent out the other units. The rental income from the other units can be used to offset your mortgage payment — and in many cases nearly covers it entirely. You're building equity and getting your housing paid for by tenants.
</p>

<h3>How We Count the Rental Income</h3>
<p>
  FHA allows us to count <strong>75% of the projected market rent</strong> from the non-owner units toward your qualifying income. We use a professional rent schedule (from the appraiser) to establish the market rent figure. This can dramatically increase how much home you qualify for.
</p>

<h3>Property Requirements</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Must be 1–4 units (no more than 4)</li>
  <li>You must occupy one unit as your primary residence for at least one year</li>
  <li>Property must meet FHA minimum property standards (HUD requires livable conditions in all units)</li>
  <li>All units must be legal and permitted (no unpermitted conversions)</li>
</ul>

<h3>Madison Market Reality</h3>
<p>
  Duplexes in the Madison area — particularly near UW campus, Willy Street, and the near east side — command strong rents. I've helped buyers purchase duplexes where their net monthly housing cost was under $500/month after rental income. It's real, and it works.
</p>
