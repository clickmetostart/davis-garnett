<p><em>Rob Miller, Branch Manager at MadCity Home Loans, explains one of the most common scenarios we see from Realtors working with out-of-state buyers coming to Madison.</em></p>

<h3>My buyer is relocating to Madison — what loan options do they have?</h3>

<p>
  Relocation buyers are one of our specialties at MadCity Home Loans. Whether your buyer is coming from Chicago, Minneapolis, or the coasts for a job at Epic Systems, UW Health, or American Family Insurance, here's how we get them approved:
</p>

<h3>Qualifying With an Offer Letter</h3>
<p>
  If your buyer hasn't started their new job yet, most loan types allow us to use a signed employment offer letter as income documentation — as long as:
</p>
<ul>
  <li>The start date is within 60–90 days of closing</li>
  <li>The salary or hourly rate is clearly stated</li>
  <li>The position is in the same field as their prior employment</li>
  <li>There are no conditions that could prevent them from starting (background checks pending, etc.)</li>
</ul>

<h3>Out-of-State Income</h3>
<p>
  If your buyer is keeping their current job and working remotely, we use their existing W-2 income as normal. The property being in Wisconsin is all that matters for loan qualification — where the buyer earns doesn't change the process.
</p>

<h3>Relocation Package Funds</h3>
<p>
  Many large employers in Madison (Epic, UW, American Family) offer relocation packages with lump-sum payments. We can document these funds for use toward a down payment or reserves, as long as we can verify the source and nature of the payment.
</p>

<h3>Timing Tip for Realtors</h3>
<p>
  Connect your relocation buyer to us as early as possible — even before they find a property. We can pre-underwrite the file with the offer letter so that when they find a home, they can close in 21–30 days without delay.
</p>
