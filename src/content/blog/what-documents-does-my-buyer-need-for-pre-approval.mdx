<p><em>Rob Miller, Branch Manager at MadCity Home Loans, gives Realtors the exact checklist to send buyers before their first call with us.</em></p>

<h3>What documents does my buyer need for a mortgage pre-approval?</h3>

<p>
  Getting your buyer's documents ready upfront is one of the fastest ways to speed up the pre-approval process and prevent last-minute surprises. Here's exactly what I need:
</p>

<h3>For W-2 Employees (Salaried or Hourly)</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Last 2 years of W-2s (from every employer)</li>
  <li>Last 30 days of pay stubs</li>
  <li>Last 2 months of bank statements (all pages, all accounts)</li>
  <li>Government-issued photo ID</li>
  <li>Social Security number (for credit pull authorization)</li>
</ul>

<h3>For Self-Employed Buyers</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Last 2 years of personal tax returns (all schedules)</li>
  <li>Last 2 years of business tax returns (if applicable)</li>
  <li>Year-to-date Profit & Loss statement</li>
  <li>Last 2 months of personal and business bank statements</li>
</ul>

<h3>For All Buyers — Additional Items as Needed</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Divorce decree / child support order (if applicable)</li>
  <li>Gift letter (if receiving gift funds for down payment)</li>
  <li>Landlord contact information (if renting and no mortgage history)</li>
  <li>DD-214 or Certificate of Eligibility (for VA loans)</li>
  <li>Proof of retirement income, Social Security, or other income sources</li>
</ul>

<h3>Pro Tip for Realtors</h3>
<p>
  Send your buyer this list <em>before</em> you introduce them to us. Buyers who show up to that first call with documents in hand get a same-day pre-approval in most cases. Buyers who don't have them can take 3–5 days longer — and in a fast market, that gap matters.
</p>
