<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Mortgage Rules for Relocating Professionals</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If you are moving to Wisconsin from another state, underwriters look at your employment status differently than a standard local buyer. Here are the three most important rules you need to know about relocation financing:</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">1. Qualifying with an Offer Letter</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">You do **not** have to wait until you receive your first paystub from Epic to buy a home. Under conventional mortgage guidelines, we can qualify you using your official **Epic Systems offer letter or employment contract**. 
The offer letter must be signed, list your start date (which typically must be within 60 to 90 days of closing), and specify your base salary. This allows you to close on your new home and move in *before* your first day of work.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">2. Navigating Relocation Packages</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Epic often provides relocation assistance or signing bonuses to incoming employees. If you plan to use relocation funds or a signing bonus toward your down payment or closing costs, those funds must be fully documented. We will need verification of the relocation agreement and proof that the funds have been deposited into your bank account before closing.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">3. Accounting for Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) and Bonuses</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Many tech and corporate professionals receive bonuses or stock options as part of their compensation package. While we can use this income to qualify you for a higher loan amount, guidelines require a **2-year history** of receiving bonuses or RSUs from your employer to include them as stable qualifying income. If you are brand new to Epic, we will qualify you based on your base salary, but we can structure your loan options to plan for future equity growth.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Top Loan Programs for Verona Home Buyers</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The right loan program depends on your down payment savings, credit profile, and the purchase price of the home. Here are the most popular financing paths for relocating professionals in Verona:</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Conventional Loans</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">With W-2 income and good credit (620+), conventional loans are the workhorse of Verona financing. You can put down as little as 3% to 5%. If you put less than 20% down, private mortgage insurance (PMI) will apply, but it falls off automatically once your loan balance reaches 80% of the home's value.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Jumbo Loans</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Many single-family homes in Verona’s premier subdivisions and surrounding acreage exceed the conforming loan limit ($832,750 in 2026). If you need to borrow more than this conforming threshold, you will need a jumbo loan. Rob Miller’s team offers highly competitive jumbo rates up to $3M+ with flexible terms, requiring as little as 10% to 15% down with no PMI options.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">WHEDA Loans & Down Payment Assistance</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If you are a first-time buyer or have moderate income, the state's <a href="/knowledge-hub/wheda-loans-wisconsin-down-payment-assistance">WHEDA</a> program offers below-market interest rates and <a href="/knowledge-hub/down-payment-assistance-madison-dane-county-wi">down payment assistance</a> loans. This allows you to purchase a home with virtually no money out of pocket.</p>
