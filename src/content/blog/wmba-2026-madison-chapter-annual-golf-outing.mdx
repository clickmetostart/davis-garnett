<p class="wp-block-paragraph">
  Rob Miller and the MadCity Home Loans team participated in the <strong>Wisconsin Mortgage Bankers Association (WMBA) Madison Chapter Annual Golf Outing</strong>, which took place on <strong>Thursday, June 4, 2026</strong>.
</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">
  Held at the beautiful <strong>Pleasant View Golf Course</strong> in Middleton, WI, this premier event brought together residential mortgage professionals, lenders, and real estate finance leaders from across the Madison area. For Rob, participating in WMBA events is a core part of supporting industry-wide cooperation and maintaining the local expertise that helps Wisconsin families achieve homeownership.
</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Event Details</h3>
<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li><strong>Event:</strong> WMBA 2026 Madison Chapter Annual Golf Outing</li>
  <li><strong>Date:</strong> Thursday, June 4, 2026</li>
  <li><strong>Location:</strong> Pleasant View Golf Course, 1322 N Pleasant View Road, Middleton, WI 53562</li>
  <li><strong>Schedule:</strong> Registration at 8:45 AM | Shotgun Start at 10:00 AM</li>
</ul>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">
  The day included 18 holes of golf with a cart, a hot lunch served \"at the turn,\" and an after-golf networking reception featuring appetizers, prizes, and announcements. As a trusted local mortgage team, we spent the day connecting with colleagues, sharing market strategies, and celebrating the Madison community.
</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">About the Wisconsin Mortgage Bankers Association</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">
  The WMBA is the only statewide organization dedicated exclusively to representing the residential real estate finance industry in Wisconsin. Their mission is to provide cooperative action in advancing the common interests of real estate finance professionals, helping to build, support, and sustain the local communities in which we all live, work, and play.
</p>
