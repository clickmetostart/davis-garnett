<p class="wp-block-paragraph">
  Rob Miller and the MadCity Home Loans team had the honor of participating in the <strong>2nd Annual Hero Centers of Wisconsin Golf Outing</strong>, which took place on <strong>Friday, August 22, 2025</strong>. 
</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">
  Held at <strong>The Bridges Golf Course</strong> in Madison, WI, this special event brought the local community together to raise crucial funds for <strong>Heroes House</strong>, an organization dedicated to providing transitional housing, counseling, and essential support services for homeless and at-risk veterans in our state. Supporting those who have served our country is a cause close to our hearts, and we are grateful to the organizers for hosting such an impactful day.
</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Outing Victory</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">
  We are incredibly proud to share that the MadCity Home Loans team not only participated but also <strong>took first place in the outing!</strong> Playing in the four-person scramble format, Rob's team put together a stellar round to finish at the top of the leaderboard. While holding up the first-place trophy was a fantastic highlight, the true reward was helping support a vital mission for local veterans.
</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Event Details</h3>
<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li><strong>Event:</strong> 2nd Annual Hero Centers of Wisconsin Golf Outing</li>
  <li><strong>Date:</strong> Friday, August 22, 2025</li>
  <li><strong>Location:</strong> The Bridges Golf Course, 2702 Shopko Dr, Madison, WI</li>
  <li><strong>Format:</strong> 4-Person Scramble</li>
  <li><strong>Beneficiary:</strong> Hero Centers of Wisconsin (Heroes House)</li>
</ul>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">
  Thanks to the participation of players, donors, and the golf course, the outing raised vital resources to assist veterans as they transition into stable, independent lives. We look forward to continuing to support and participate in community initiatives that honor our local heroes.
</p>
