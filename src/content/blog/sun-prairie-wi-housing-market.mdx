<p><em>Rob Miller, Branch Manager at MadCity Home Loans, answers one of the most common questions we get from buyers looking at the East Side.</em></p>

<h3>What is the Sun Prairie, WI housing market like?</h3>

<p>
  Sun Prairie is no longer just a "sleepy suburb" east of Madison—it is one of the fastest-growing municipalities in the entire state of Wisconsin. For homebuyers, this explosive growth brings both incredible opportunities and some unique challenges.
</p>
<p>
  Here is what you need to know if you are trying to buy a house in Sun Prairie right now:
</p>

<h3>1. You Get More Bang for Your Buck (Relatively Speaking)</h3>
<p>
  If you are comparing Sun Prairie to west-side suburbs like Middleton or Verona, your dollar is going to stretch a bit further here. You can often find newer construction, larger lot sizes, and more square footage for the same purchase price. 
</p>
<p>
  This makes Sun Prairie incredibly popular with first-time homebuyers and growing families who are feeling priced out of the Madison city limits.
</p>

<h3>2. The "New Construction" Factor</h3>
<p>
  Sun Prairie has seen massive residential development over the last decade. If you are interested in building a new home or buying a house that is less than 5 years old, Sun Prairie has significantly more inventory than most other Dane County suburbs.
</p>
<p>
  However, remember that new construction often comes with "special assessments" for things like new roads or sewer lines, which can impact your property taxes and monthly payment.
</p>

<h3>3. The Commute and Infrastructure</h3>
<p>
  Sun Prairie has built up its commercial infrastructure so well (grocery stores, restaurants, shopping) that many residents rarely feel the need to drive into Madison. 
</p>
<p>
  If you <em>do</em> commute to downtown Madison or the UW Campus, you will rely heavily on Highway 151. During peak rush hour, this corridor can bottleneck, so I always advise buyers to do a "practice commute" on a Tuesday morning before writing an offer.
</p>

<h3>4. Property Taxes in Sun Prairie</h3>
<p>
  This is where my team comes in. Because Sun Prairie is building so many new schools and city facilities to keep up with the population boom, property taxes can be slightly higher than in some older, more established municipalities. 
</p>
<p>
  When we issue your pre-approval, we will specifically run the numbers using Sun Prairie's current tax rates so there are zero surprises when you get your final monthly payment calculation.
</p>
