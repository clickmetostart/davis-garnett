<p><em>Rob Miller, Branch Manager at MadCity Home Loans, answers one of the most common questions we get from buyers looking South of Madison.</em></p>

<h3>What is the Fitchburg, WI housing market like?</h3>

<p>
  Fitchburg is perhaps the most unique suburb in Dane County. Instead of being a distinct town separated by miles of highway, Fitchburg physically wraps around the southern edge of Madison. It seamlessly blends urban convenience with sprawling rural landscapes.
</p>
<p>
  Here is what you need to know about buying a home in Fitchburg right now:
</p>

<h3>1. Unbeatable Proximity</h3>
<p>
  Fitchburg's biggest selling point is its location. If you work in downtown Madison, at the UW Campus, or on the Beltline, living in Fitchburg often provides a faster, easier commute than living in Madison itself. 
</p>

<h3>2. The "Hybrid" City</h3>
<p>
  Fitchburg is incredibly diverse in its housing stock. In the northern part of the city (closer to the Beltline), you will find higher-density neighborhoods, apartment complexes, and starter homes that blend right into Madison. As you move south, the city opens up into sprawling, high-end subdivisions, large acreage lots, and active farmland. 
</p>
<p>
  This means Fitchburg truly has something for every price point, from first-time homebuyers to luxury buyers.
</p>

<h3>3. No Traditional "Downtown"</h3>
<p>
  Because Fitchburg is so spread out and integrated into Madison, it lacks a centralized, walkable "Main Street" downtown area like you find in Middleton, Verona, or Waunakee. While it has great commercial zones (like the Hatchery Hill corridor), it doesn't have a traditional town square feel.
</p>

<h3>4. The Three School Districts</h3>
<p>
  This is the most critical thing to know if you have kids: Fitchburg does not have its own school district. Depending on where you buy a house in Fitchburg, your children will go to either the Madison Metropolitan School District, the Verona Area School District, or the Oregon School District.
</p>
<p>
  This drastically affects property values and property taxes. A house in Fitchburg zoned for Verona schools will typically command a higher price than a nearly identical house in Fitchburg zoned for Madison schools. My team always verifies the school zoning when we run your estimated mortgage payment to ensure the taxes are calculated correctly.
</p>
