<p class="wp-block-paragraph">
  I am incredibly excited to once again support the UW-Whitewater Cross Country and Track & Field programs at their <strong>13th annual golf outing</strong> on <strong>Friday, July 24, 2026</strong>, at Evergreen Golf Course in Elkhorn. 
</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">
  Although UW-Whitewater isn't my alma mater, after being invited to this event a few years back, I’ve come to truly enjoy how well-run it is. It's a fantastic way to benefit these incredible student-athletes, and of course, it provides a great excuse to hit the links on a Friday!
</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Event Details</h3>
<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li><strong>Date:</strong> Friday, July 24, 2026</li>
  <li><strong>Location:</strong> Evergreen Golf Course, Elkhorn, WI</li>
  <li><strong>Time:</strong> Registration begins at 9:45 a.m. | Shotgun start at 11:00 a.m.</li>
  <li><strong>Entry Fee:</strong> $130 per person (includes 18 holes of golf with cart and dinner). Non-golfers are welcome to attend the dinner for $30.</li>
</ul>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">
  Alumni, spouses, parents, family, and friends of the program are encouraged to sign up, with or without a complete foursome. For more information, contact head cross country coach Jeff Miller at millerj@uww.edu or 262-472-5648, or head track and field coach Mike Johnson at JohnsonMJ03@uww.edu or 262-472-1367.
</p>
