<p class="wp-block-paragraph">
  Rob Miller and the MadCity Home Loans team are excited to participate in and attend the annual <strong>Firefighters Charity Outing</strong> scheduled for <strong>Monday, June 29, 2026</strong>. For Rob, working with the community and our strategic partners is all about building strong local connections, making these outings feel like a warm gathering of friends and family.
</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">
  This annual event brings together local businesses, community members, and first responders for a day of golf and community building, with all proceeds directly benefiting the <strong>Milwaukee Firefighters Local 215</strong> charitable organizations and community partners.
</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Event Details</h3>
<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li><strong>Event:</strong> Firefighters Charity Golf Outing</li>
  <li><strong>Date:</strong> Monday, June 29, 2026</li>
  <li><strong>Beneficiary:</strong> Milwaukee Firefighters Local 215 Charitable Funds</li>
</ul>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">
  Our first responders put their lives on the line daily for our communities. This outing is a wonderful opportunity to show our gratitude, catch up with neighbors, and stand alongside the first responders who protect us every day.
</p>
