<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What is a WHEDA Loan?</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">WHEDA is a state-chartered authority designed to make homeownership more accessible and affordable for low-to-moderate-income buyers in Wisconsin. WHEDA does not lend money directly to buyers; instead, they partner with approved local lenders and brokers who originate, process, and fund the loans under WHEDA's guidelines.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">WHEDA home loans offer below-market interest rates, reduced mortgage insurance premiums, and flexible qualifying guidelines. Most importantly, WHEDA offers secondary loans specifically designed to cover your down payment and closing costs.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Rob Miller’s team specializes in matching eligible buyers with the correct WHEDA product. Learn more about WHEDA options.</p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Two Main WHEDA First Mortgage Programs</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">WHEDA offers two primary first-mortgage options, depending on your financial profile and preferences:</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">1. WHEDA Advantage Conventional</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This is WHEDA's flagship program and is often a superior alternative to standard conventional loans. It requires a minimum credit score of 620 and offers several unique advantages:</p>
<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li><strong>Low Down Payment</strong>: Only 3% down payment required (which can be fully covered by WHEDA's down payment assistance).</li>
  <li><strong>Reduced Mortgage Insurance</strong>: Private mortgage insurance (PMI) premiums are significantly lower than standard conventional loans, reducing your monthly payment.</li>
  <li><strong>No PMI at 20% Equity</strong>: Unlike FHA loans, the mortgage insurance on a WHEDA Conventional loan falls off automatically once you reach 20% equity.</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">2. WHEDA Advantage FHA</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">For buyers with lower credit scores or higher debt-to-income ratios, the WHEDA Advantage FHA loan provides a more flexible path to homeownership. It requires 3.5% down and features WHEDA's competitive interest rates combined with FHA's lenient credit requirements.</p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">WHEDA Down Payment Assistance (DPA) Options</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This is where the magic happens. WHEDA allows you to pair your first mortgage with a second, low-interest loan to cover your down payment and closing costs. This means you can purchase a home with virtually **zero dollars of your own savings**.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">WHEDA currently offers two main down payment assistance options:</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">1. WHEDA Easy Close DPA</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The Easy Close program is a secondary loan that provides up to 6% of your purchase price to cover your down payment and closing costs. 
Key details include:</p>
<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li><strong>Term</strong>: A 10-year loan term.</li>
  <li><strong>Interest Rate</strong>: The interest rate matches your first mortgage rate.</li>
  <li><strong>Flexible Use</strong>: Can be used for the down payment, closing costs, or prepaids (escrows).</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">2. WHEDA Capital Access DPA</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">For buyers who meet stricter income guidelines, the Capital Access program offers up to $3,050 in assistance. 
The best part? <strong>It features a 0% interest rate and has no monthly payments.</strong> You only pay back the loan when you sell the home, <a href="/knowledge-hub/refinancing-guide-rob-miller-provisor">refinance</a> the first mortgage, or pay off the first mortgage. This is essentially interest-free, deferred-payment assistance.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If you are a first-time buyer, discover how to structure these programs.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Who Qualifies for a WHEDA Loan? The Key Requirements</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Because WHEDA is a state assistance program designed to help specific buyer populations, there are three primary qualifying criteria you must meet:</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">1. Credit Score Requirement</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">For most WHEDA programs, you need a minimum credit score of <strong>620</strong>. If your credit score is below 620, Rob Miller's team can help you build a plan to improve your credit profile through a free credit consultation before applying.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">2. Income Limits</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">WHEDA limits eligibility based on the combined household income of the borrowers. These limits are calculated by county and by household size.
For example, in **Dane County** (which includes Madison, Sun Prairie, Verona, Waunakee, etc.), the income limits are highly generous to account for the local cost of living. In many cases, households earning up to $100,000+ can still qualify for WHEDA programs.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">3. Home Buyer Education</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">To ensure long-term homeownership success, WHEDA requires all borrowers to complete a state-approved home buyer education course before closing. This course can be completed online or in person, and it covers budgeting, the mortgage process, and home maintenance. Rob's team regularly hosts live webinars and provides resources to help buyers fulfill this requirement easily.</p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Is a WHEDA Loan Right for You? WHEDA vs. Conventional vs. FHA</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">While WHEDA is an incredible program, it isn't always the best fit for every buyer. Here is a quick look at how WHEDA stacks up against standard loan options:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li><strong>Choose WHEDA if</strong>: You have good credit (620+) but lack the savings for a down payment and closing costs, and your income falls under the local county limits. Pairing a WHEDA mortgage with Easy Close DPA is the fastest way to get into a home without saving for years.</li>
  <li><strong>Choose Standard Conventional if</strong>: You have a credit score of 720+, have saved at least 3% to 5% of your own down payment, and want to avoid the minor interest and fees associated with a secondary DPA loan. Explore conventional loans →</li>
  <li><strong>Choose FHA if</strong>: Your credit score is below 620 (FHA allows scores down to 580 with 3.5% down) or your income exceeds the WHEDA county limits but you still need a low down payment option. Explore FHA loans →</li>
</ul>
