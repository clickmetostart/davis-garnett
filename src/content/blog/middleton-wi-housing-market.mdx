<p><em>Rob Miller, Branch Manager at MadCity Home Loans, answers one of the most common questions we get from buyers looking at the West Side.</em></p>

<h3>What is the Middleton, WI housing market like?</h3>

<p>
  Middleton is consistently ranked as one of the best places to live in the United States, and the housing market reflects that reputation. Also known as the "Good Neighbor City," Middleton borders the west side of Madison and Lake Mendota.
</p>
<p>
  If you are trying to buy a house in Middleton, here is the reality of what you are walking into:
</p>

<h3>1. High Demand, Low Inventory</h3>
<p>
  Middleton is one of the most competitive markets in Dane County. The combination of a top-tier school district, incredible parks (like the Pheasant Branch Conservancy), and a vibrant downtown area means homes rarely sit on the market for long. 
</p>
<p>
  You absolutely must have your <a href="/knowledge-hub/mortgage-pre-approval-madison-wi">pre-approval fully underwritten</a> before you start looking. If you wait to get pre-approved until after you see a house you like in Middleton, it will likely be under contract before you get your paperwork submitted.
</p>

<h3>2. The Price Point</h3>
<p>
  Be prepared: your purchasing power does not stretch as far in Middleton as it does in suburbs to the east or north (like Sun Prairie or DeForest). The median home price in Middleton is significantly higher than the Dane County average. You are paying a premium for the location and the school district.
</p>

<h3>3. Property Taxes</h3>
<p>
  Because of the high-quality schools and extensive city services, property taxes in Middleton are on the higher end of the spectrum for Dane County. When my team runs your numbers, we always factor in the specific Middleton tax mill rate so you know exactly what your total monthly payment will be, not just the principal and interest.
</p>

<h3>4. How to Win an Offer Here</h3>
<p>
  To win a house in Middleton, you need a flawless offer. This means a localized pre-approval letter from a lender the listing agent recognizes and trusts (like MadCity Home Loans). A pre-approval letter from an out-of-state online lender often gets pushed to the bottom of the pile in competitive Middleton situations.
</p>
