<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Step 2: Choose the Right Loan Program for Your Situation</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">One of Rob Miller's greatest strengths is his deep command of every loan program available to Wisconsin buyers. There is no one-size-fits-all mortgage — and the right program depends on your down payment, credit, income type, and the property itself.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Here's a breakdown of the most popular programs for Madison-area buyers:</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Conventional Loans</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The workhorse of the mortgage world. Conventional loans are ideal for buyers with solid credit (typically 620+) and at least 3–5% to put down. Once you reach 20% equity, private mortgage insurance drops off automatically — no <a href="/knowledge-hub/refinancing-guide-rob-miller-provisor">refinance</a> required.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">FHA Loans</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">With down payments as low as 3.5% and flexible credit qualification, FHA loans are a strong option for first-time buyers and those rebuilding credit. This is one of the most popular programs for buyers entering the Madison market for the first time.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">VA Loans</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If you're a veteran, active-duty military member, or qualifying surviving spouse, you may be eligible for a <a href="/knowledge-hub/va-benefits-and-options">VA loan</a> with <strong>zero down payment and no PMI</strong>. Rob holds the Military Lending Outreach Professional (MLOP) and Certified Military Housing Specialist (CMHS) designations — meaning he is specifically trained to maximize VA benefits for those who've served.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">USDA Loans</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Several communities surrounding Madison — including parts of DeForest, Oregon, and Waunakee — fall within USDA-eligible rural zones. USDA loans offer zero down payment financing with competitive rates for qualifying buyers and properties. Check your eligibility today.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">WHEDA Loans</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The Wisconsin Housing and Economic Development Authority's WHEDA loan program is Wisconsin's own below-market interest rate and <a href="/knowledge-hub/down-payment-assistance-madison-dane-county-wi">down payment assistance</a> program. If you're a low-to-moderate income buyer purchasing in Wisconsin, this program can dramatically reduce your upfront costs and monthly payment. It's one of the most underutilized programs in the state — and Rob's team knows how to maximize it.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Jumbo Loans</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Buying a higher-value home in Middleton, Waunakee, or one of Madison's premier lakeside neighborhoods? If your loan amount exceeds the 2026 conforming limit of $832,750, you'll need a jumbo loan. Rob's team offers jumbo financing up to $3M+ with competitive fixed and adjustable rate options.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Non-Conventional / Non-QM Loans</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Self-employed? Commission-based income? Real estate investor? Rob's non-conventional loan programs include bank statement loans, DSCR investor products, and asset-depletion programs for borrowers who don't fit the traditional W-2 mold.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Step 3: Understand the Madison-Area Communities You're Shopping In</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Greater Madison isn't one market — it's many. Here's a quick guide to what buyers can expect in some of the most popular communities:</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Sun Prairie</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">One of Dane County's fastest-growing cities, Sun Prairie offers a mix of affordable starter homes, newer subdivisions, and established neighborhoods with strong schools and easy I-94 access to Madison. A great fit for first-time buyers and growing families.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Waunakee</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">"The Only Waunakee in the World" offers top-rated schools, spacious lots, and a tight-knit community feel. Home values trend higher here, making conventional and jumbo financing common. Rob's team regularly works with buyers relocating to Waunakee for its school district.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Middleton</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Just west of Madison, Middleton consistently ranks among Wisconsin's best places to live. Expect strong competition and homes that move fast. Pre-approval is non-negotiable in this market.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Verona</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Home to Epic Systems (one of the largest private employers in Wisconsin), Verona attracts a large professional buyer pool. Newer construction is plentiful, and one-time close construction financing is an option worth exploring for buyers building new.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">DeForest</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A growing community north of Madison with newer neighborhoods, strong schools, and more affordable price points than many western suburbs. USDA eligibility may apply to some DeForest properties — worth checking with Rob's team.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Oregon</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Charming small-town feel with a loyal community and solid school system, Oregon sits south of Madison on the edge of rural Dane County. Some properties in Oregon-area zip codes qualify for USDA zero-down financing.</p>
