<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Buying a home is one of the biggest financial decisions of your life — and it involves a lot of moving parts. From checking your credit to changing your locks on move-in day, the details matter. Rob Miller, Branch Manager and Senior Loan Officer at MadCity Home Loans powered by ProVisor, Inc., has helped thousands of Wisconsin buyers navigate the process without missing a step.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This comprehensive checklist breaks the entire home buying journey into manageable phases. Work through it from top to bottom, check things off as you go, and you'll approach every stage of the process with confidence and clarity.</p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Phase 1: Pre-Purchase Preparation</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This is where everything starts — before you look at a single listing. Getting your financial foundation right makes everything that follows faster, smoother, and less stressful.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Determine Your Budget</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Build an honest monthly budget that accounts for all your current income and expenses. What's comfortably left over each month? That's the ceiling for your mortgage payment — and it's more meaningful than the maximum a lender will approve you for. A good rule of thumb: your total housing costs (mortgage, taxes, insurance) should be no more than 28% of your gross monthly income.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Check Your Credit Score</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Pull your credit reports from all three bureaus (Equifax, Experian, TransUnion) at annualcreditreport.com. Review for errors and dispute anything inaccurate. Your credit score directly affects your mortgage rate — even a modest improvement can save you thousands over the life of a loan. Rob Miller's team offers a free credit consultation with honest, actionable guidance.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Save for a Down Payment</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Down payment requirements in Wisconsin vary by loan type:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li><strong>VA and USDA loans:</strong> 0% down (for eligible buyers)</li>
  <li><strong>FHA loans:</strong> 3.5% down</li>
  <li><strong>Conventional loans:</strong> 3–5% down (PMI may apply below 20%)</li>
  <li><strong>Conventional with no PMI:</strong> 20% down</li>
</ul>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">There are also <a href="/knowledge-hub/down-payment-assistance-madison-dane-county-wi">down payment assistance</a> programs available in Wisconsin through <a href="/knowledge-hub/wheda-loans-wisconsin-down-payment-assistance">WHEDA</a> and local municipalities. Rob Miller's team researches all available programs for every buyer — this is part of the service, not an add-on.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Build an Emergency Fund</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Before closing on a home, make sure you have 3–6 months of living expenses saved separately from your down payment. Homeownership comes with unexpected costs — HVAC repairs, roof leaks, appliance failures — and a cushion prevents those surprises from becoming crises.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Research Mortgage Options</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Familiarize yourself with the basic loan types available to Wisconsin buyers:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li><strong>Conventional:</strong> Most common, best for buyers with solid credit and some down payment</li>
  <li><strong>FHA:</strong> Lower credit and down payment requirements, backed by the federal government</li>
  <li><strong>VA:</strong> 0% down, no PMI, for eligible veterans and active-duty service members</li>
  <li><strong>USDA:</strong> 0% down for qualifying rural and suburban areas</li>
  <li><strong>WHEDA:</strong> Wisconsin-specific programs with competitive rates for qualifying buyers</li>
</ul>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Rob Miller walks every buyer through all applicable options and recommends the program that best fits their situation — not the one that's easiest to process.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Get Pre-Approved</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="/knowledge-hub/mortgage-pre-approval-madison-wi">Pre-approval</a> is your starting gun. It tells you exactly what you can afford, gives you a letter to submit with offers, and signals to sellers that your financing is already verified. Rob Miller's pre-approvals are trusted by Madison-area Realtors because his closings happen on time. Get started with pre-approval →</p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Phase 2: House Hunting</h2>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Define Your Priorities</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Before you walk into a single showing, build a clear list of what you need vs. what you want. Consider: location, school district, commute, number of bedrooms/bathrooms, yard size, garage, neighborhood walkability, and proximity to family or amenities.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Choose a Trusted Real Estate Agent</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A skilled Realtor is your advocate, your market expert, and your negotiator. They know what homes are worth, what sellers will accept, and how to protect your interests through every phase. If you need a Realtor recommendation for the Madison area, Rob Miller's team can connect you with trusted partners who share the same commitment to a smooth, client-first experience.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Visit Multiple Properties</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Don't fall in love with the first home you see. Visit a range of properties at different price points and in different neighborhoods before narrowing your focus. Take notes, photos, and ask your Realtor for honest feedback on each home — including any red flags.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Consider Future Resale Value</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Even if you plan to stay long-term, you'll eventually sell. Location, school district, lot size, and neighborhood trajectory all affect long-term value. Your Realtor can walk you through recent appreciation trends in the neighborhoods you're considering.</p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Phase 3: Making an Offer</h2>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Research Comparable Homes</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Your Realtor will pull comparable sales ("comps") from the same neighborhood — homes of similar size, age, and condition that have recently sold. This data drives your offer price and prevents you from overpaying.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Determine Your Offer Strategy</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In competitive markets, your first offer may need to be strong. In slower markets, there may be room to start below asking price. Lean on your Realtor's current market knowledge to structure an offer that wins without leaving money on the table.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Submit an Earnest Money Deposit</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Earnest money (typically 1–2% of purchase price in Wisconsin) is deposited into escrow when your offer is accepted. It shows the seller you're serious and is credited toward your down payment or closing costs at closing. If the deal falls through due to a contingency, earnest money is typically returned.</p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Phase 4: Post-Offer Tasks</h2>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Schedule a Home Inspection</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Hire a licensed, experienced inspector — not a bargain option. Attend the inspection in person. Learn about your home's systems, maintenance needs, and any issues that may require negotiation with the seller.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Negotiate Repairs or Credits</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If the inspection reveals significant issues, work with your Realtor to request repairs, price reductions, or seller credits toward closing costs. Rob Miller's team coordinates closely with your Realtor during this phase to make sure loan conditions align with any agreed-upon changes.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Finalize Your Mortgage</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Submit all required documents to Rob Miller's team promptly — delays in document collection are the most common cause of closing timeline slippage. Stay responsive and organized, and the rest of the process runs smoothly.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Review All Contracts</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Read everything before you sign. Your Realtor and closing attorney (if applicable in Wisconsin) should walk you through each document. If something doesn't make sense, ask. Rob Miller's team is also available to explain any loan-related documents in plain language.</p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Phase 5: Closing</h2>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Review Your Closing Disclosure</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Three business days before closing, your Closing Disclosure arrives with final loan terms, payment amounts, and all costs. Rob Miller's team reviews this with every client — compare it to your Loan Estimate and flag any discrepancies immediately.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Arrange Payment for Closing Costs</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Closing costs typically require a cashier's check or wire transfer — personal checks are not accepted. Confirm the exact amount and wire instructions with your title company or attorney at least 24 hours before closing.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Do a Final Walkthrough</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Walk through the home one last time before the closing appointment. Confirm the property is in the agreed-upon condition, all negotiated repairs have been completed, and the seller has fully vacated.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Sign and Close</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Bring your photo ID, certified funds, and proof of homeowners insurance. Sign your documents, receive your keys, and take a deep breath — you're officially a homeowner.</p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Phase 6: Post-Purchase</h2>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Change the Locks</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Do this on day one. You don't know who has copies of the existing keys — previous owners, contractors, neighbors, or real estate agents. Rekeying all exterior locks is inexpensive and non-negotiable.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Update Your Address Everywhere</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">File your USPS change of address, then update directly with your bank, employer, the IRS, Wisconsin DMV, insurance providers, doctors, credit card companies, and anyone else who sends you mail or holds your address on file.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Meet Your Neighbors</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A friendly relationship with your neighbors pays dividends — they'll look out for your property, inform you of neighborhood issues, and generally make your new home feel like a community.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">✅ Plan for Ongoing Maintenance</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Set aside 1% of your home's value annually for maintenance and repairs. Create a seasonal maintenance calendar covering HVAC filter changes, gutter cleaning, water heater flushing, and other routine tasks that prevent small problems from becoming expensive emergencies.</p>
