<h3 class="wp-block-heading">DO:</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Availability – Keep your financial records close at hand in case updates are requested.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Income – Be aware that underwriters typically verify your income and tax documents through your employer, CPA, and/or IRS tax transcripts. Hold onto new pay stubs as received.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Assets – Continue saving incoming account statements. Keep all numbered pages of each statement.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Gifts – If you’re receiving any gift money from relatives, they’ll need to sign a gift letter (we’ll provide) and an account statement evidencing the source, which must be “seasoned” funds.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Current Residence – If you’re renting, continue paying your rent on time and save proof of payment. If you’re selling your current residence, be prepared to show your HUD-1 Settlement Statement. If you’ll be renting your home, you may need to show sufficient equity, a lease and receipt of the first month’s rent and security deposit.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Keep Your Credit Shining – Continue making payments on time. Your credit report may be pulled again, and any negative change to your score could cause you to lose your approval and your home.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Understand that Things Have Changed – Underwriters require more documentation than in the past. Even if requests seem silly, intrusive or unnecessary, please remember that if they didn’t need it, they wouldn’t ask.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">DON’T:</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Apply for New Credit – Changes in credit can cause delays, change the terms of your financing or even prevent closing. If you must open a new account (or even borrow against retirement funds), please consult with me first.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Change Jobs During the Process – Probationary periods, career or even status changes (such as from a salaried to a commissioned position, leave of absence or new bonus structure) can be subject to very strict rules.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Make Undocumented Deposits – Primarily for large but sometimes even small deposits must be sourced unless they are identified. Make copies of check and deposit slips. Keep your deposits separate and small. Avoid depositing cash.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Wait to Liquidate Funds from Stick or Retirement Accounts – If you need to sell investments, do it now and document the transaction. Don’t take the risk that the market could move against you leaving you short of funds to close.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Never Be Afraid to Ask Questions — If you're uncertain about what you need or what you should do, contact Rob Miller to help you through the process.</p>
