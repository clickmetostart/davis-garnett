<p class="wp-block-paragraph">
  Rob Miller and the MadCity Home Loans team are honored to participate in and attend the upcoming <strong>Neumy Memorial Golf Outing</strong> on <strong>Saturday, July 18, 2026</strong>. Rob truly loves working alongside his community and strategic partners to support these local events, which always feel like a welcoming gathering of friends and family.
</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">
  This annual memorial event raises vital funds for <strong>Agrace Hospice Care</strong>, supporting their patient care fund, specialized grief support services, and community educational programs across Southern Wisconsin.
</p>

We look forward to seeing many familiar faces on the course!
