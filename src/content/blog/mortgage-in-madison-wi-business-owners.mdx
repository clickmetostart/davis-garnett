<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Business owners often use legal strategies to reduce taxable income, including:</p>

<li>Operating expenses</li>

<li>Marketing costs</li>

<ul class="wp-block-list">

<li>Bank statements</li>



<li>Business financials</li>
