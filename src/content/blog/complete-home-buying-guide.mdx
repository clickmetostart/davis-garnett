<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Buying a home is one of the most meaningful financial decisions you'll ever make — and one of the most exciting. But excitement alone doesn't get you across the finish line. You need a plan, a trusted team, and a clear understanding of what's ahead at each stage of the process.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Rob Miller, Branch Manager and Senior Loan Officer at MadCity Home Loans powered by ProVisor, Inc., built this guide to walk Wisconsin buyers through the entire journey: from asking yourself the right questions at the beginning, to celebrating in your new home at the end. Let's walk through it together.</p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">5 Reasons Homeownership Is Worth It</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Before we talk about the process, let's talk about the payoff. Buying a home isn't just about having a place to live — it's one of the most powerful tools for building long-term financial stability:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li><strong>Equity Building:</strong> Every mortgage payment reduces what you owe and increases what you own. Unlike rent — which builds someone else's equity — your mortgage builds yours. Over time, that equity becomes a significant financial asset you can tap, leverage, or cash out.</li>
  <li><strong>Freedom and Personalization:</strong> You own it. Paint it, remodel it, landscape it, gut the kitchen — the home becomes a reflection of your taste, your family's needs, and your life.</li>
  <li><strong>Tax Advantages:</strong> The U.S. Tax Code allows deductions for mortgage interest, property taxes, and other homeownership costs. Talk to a CPA about how this applies to your specific situation — the savings can be meaningful.</li>
  <li><strong>Appreciation:</strong> Real estate values have historically increased over the long term. In Wisconsin's strong markets — Madison, Verona, Middleton, Sun Prairie, Fitchburg — appreciation has been particularly consistent.</li>
  <li><strong>Community and Stability:</strong> Owning a home allows you to put down roots, build connections, and create a stable environment for your family for years to come.</li>
</ul>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Don't Disqualify Yourself Before You Start</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">One of the most common mistakes Rob Miller sees is buyers ruling themselves out before they've even talked to a lender. There's a lot of outdated information circulating about what it takes to qualify for a home loan. Let's clear up the biggest myths right now:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li><strong>Myth: You need perfect credit.</strong> False. FHA loans are available to buyers with scores as low as 580. VA and USDA loans have flexible credit standards as well.</li>
  <li><strong>Myth: You need a 20% down payment.</strong> False. Conventional loans are available with as little as 3% down. FHA requires 3.5%. VA and USDA loans are 0% down for eligible buyers.</li>
  <li><strong>Myth: You can't qualify if you have student loans.</strong> False. Student loan debt is factored into your debt-to-income ratio — it doesn't automatically disqualify you. Rob's team works with borrowers carrying student loan balances every day.</li>
</ul>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The only way to know for certain whether you qualify — and what your options are — is to have a real, honest conversation with a lender. Rob Miller's consultations are always free and completely confidential.</p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Questions to Ask Yourself Before You Buy</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Buying a home is an emotional decision — but it needs to be grounded in honest self-assessment. Before you start browsing listings, take time to answer these questions:</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">What's Your "Why"?</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Why do you want to buy a home — and why now? Is it financial (building equity vs. paying rent)? Is it lifestyle (more space, a yard, a better school district)? Is it family (room to grow, stability for your kids)? Knowing your "why" helps you prioritize what matters most in your home search — and keeps you grounded when emotions run high.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Do You Have Time for Home Maintenance?</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Homeownership comes with responsibility. HVAC filters, gutters, lawn care, appliance upkeep — these are real time and money commitments. If you're not ready for that, a condo or townhome with an HOA that handles exterior maintenance might be a better fit than a single-family home.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Do You Have an Emergency Fund?</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Unexpected expenses happen — especially with a home. Rob Miller's standard recommendation: have 3–6 months of living expenses set aside <em>in addition to</em> your down payment and closing costs. Don't drain your savings to get into the house; you'll need a cushion once you're in.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Where Does Your Credit Stand?</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Your credit score drives your mortgage rate — and your rate drives your monthly payment. Even a small improvement in your score can translate to thousands of dollars in savings over the life of a loan. Not sure where you stand? Rob Miller offers a free credit consultation that gives you an honest picture and a clear improvement plan.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Can You Handle the Upfront Costs?</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Down payments in Wisconsin typically range from 0% (VA and USDA loans) to 3–5% (conventional and FHA) to 20% (conventional with no PMI). On top of that, closing costs typically run 2–5% of the purchase price. Rob Miller's team always gives you a clear breakdown of total upfront costs before you commit to anything.</p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Create Your Budget</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">One of the most valuable things you can do before talking to a lender is build an honest monthly budget. List every source of income and every recurring expense. What's left over each month? That's the range you have to work with for a mortgage payment — and it's more important than the maximum loan amount a lender approves you for.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Rob Miller's philosophy: <strong>the right mortgage fits your life, not the other way around.</strong> Being approved for $400,000 doesn't mean you should spend $400,000. The goal is a payment that lets you live comfortably, save for retirement, handle emergencies, and still enjoy your life.</p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Build Your Home Wish List</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Before you start house hunting in earnest, create two lists:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li><strong>Must-haves:</strong> Non-negotiables. Number of bedrooms, school district, commute range, garage, accessibility needs, etc.</li>
  <li><strong>Nice-to-haves:</strong> Things you'd love but can live without. Home office, finished basement, large backyard, specific neighborhood, updated kitchen, etc.</li>
</ul>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Prioritizing this list prevents you from falling in love with a house that doesn't actually work for your family — and helps your Realtor show you homes that fit.</p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Get Pre-Approved</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Rob Miller's team delivers <a href="/knowledge-hub/mortgage-pre-approval-madison-wi">pre-approvals</a> that Madison-area Realtors trust and rely on. Here's what happens during pre-approval:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li>Your income, assets, debts, and credit profile are reviewed</li>
  <li>You receive a maximum loan amount you're approved for</li>
  <li>You receive a pre-approval letter to submit with your offer</li>
  <li>Rob's team explains every loan program available to you — conventional, FHA, VA, USDA, <a href="/knowledge-hub/wheda-loans-wisconsin-down-payment-assistance">WHEDA</a> — and recommends the best fit</li>
</ul>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">After pre-approval, Rob provides a <strong>Good Faith Estimate</strong> — a detailed breakdown of loan terms and expected costs. Compare this to your budget to make sure everything lines up.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Keep Your Finances Stable During the Process</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">From the time you get pre-approved to the day you close, it's critical to keep your financial profile consistent. Avoid these major changes:</p>
<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li>❌ Opening new credit accounts or applying for new credit cards</li>
  <li>❌ Making large purchases on credit (like buying new furniture or a car before closing)</li>
  <li>❌ Making large, undocumented deposits into your bank accounts</li>
  <li>❌ Changing jobs or switching from a W-2 position to self-employment</li>
  <li>❌ Co-signing on loans for family members or friends</li>
</ul>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Your underwriter checks your credit and employment status right before closing, and any changes can cause delays or jeopardize your loan approval. When in doubt, ask Rob Miller first.</p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Working with a Real Estate Agent</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A great Realtor is your navigator through the home buying process. Their local expertise, negotiation skills, and knowledge of the market can make a significant difference — especially in competitive Wisconsin markets where well-priced homes move fast.</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li>Ask Rob Miller's team for a Realtor recommendation — they work with some of Madison's best</li>
  <li>Sign a buyer's agreement with your agent (standard practice, protects your interests)</li>
  <li>Be specific about your wish list so they can filter effectively</li>
  <li>Communicate regularly — the more your agent knows about your preferences and timeline, the better they can serve you</li>
</ul>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Making an Offer</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">When you find the right home, your Realtor helps you structure a winning offer. Key components:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li><strong>Offer price</strong> — based on comparable sales your Realtor researches in the neighborhood</li>
  <li><strong>Earnest money deposit</strong> — held in escrow, shows good faith, credited at closing</li>
  <li><strong>Contingencies</strong> — inspection, appraisal, and financing contingencies protect your earnest money if things fall through</li>
  <li><strong>Pre-approval letter</strong> — attach Rob Miller's pre-approval letter; it signals to sellers that your financing is solid</li>
  <li><strong>Offer expiration</strong> — don't leave your offer on the table indefinitely; include a clear response deadline</li>
</ul>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Once your offer is accepted, you'll receive the <strong>Seller's Disclosure</strong> — a required document where the seller discloses any known issues with the property. Review this carefully with your Realtor. You have a window to back out if significant issues are disclosed.</p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Loan Processing & Underwriting Stage</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Once your offer is accepted, you go under contract. Rob Miller's processing team will work with you to submit your final mortgage application and gather updated documentation. Staying organized and responsive at this stage is key to keeping your closing date on track.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Common Documents You'll Need</h3>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li>Most recent 30 days of pay stubs</li>
  <li>Last 2 years of W-2s (and tax returns if self-employed)</li>
  <li>Last 2 months of bank statements (all pages, even if blank)</li>
  <li>Government-issued photo ID</li>
  <li>Recent statements for any retirement, investment, or 401(k) accounts</li>
</ul>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Home Inspection: Don't Skip It</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A professional home inspection is one of the best investments you'll make in the buying process. A qualified inspector examines the home's structure, roof, foundation, electrical systems, plumbing, HVAC, and more.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Rob Miller's recommendations:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li>Attend the inspection in person — ask questions, take notes, learn about your new home</li>
  <li>If major issues are found, use your inspection contingency to negotiate repairs or credits, or walk away without losing your earnest money</li>
  <li>Don't try to save money by skipping or shortcutting the inspector — a few hundred dollars upfront can prevent tens of thousands in surprises later</li>
</ul>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Appraisal</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Your lender orders an appraisal to confirm the home's market value supports the purchase price. The appraiser evaluates the property's age, condition, amenities, neighborhood, and comparable sales.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If the appraisal comes in lower than the purchase price, Rob Miller's team works through your options — negotiating a price reduction, covering the gap, or in some cases, revisiting the appraisal. You're never left to figure it out alone.</p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Closing Day</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">You're almost there. Bring to closing:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li>Government-issued photo ID</li>
  <li>Cashier's check or confirmed wire for down payment and closing costs</li>
  <li>Proof of homeowners insurance</li>
  <li>Copy of your purchase contract</li>
  <li>Home inspection report</li>
</ul>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Review your <strong>Closing Disclosure</strong> carefully — it should closely match your Good Faith Estimate. Rob Miller's team reviews the CD with every client before closing day. If anything looks different, flag it immediately.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Do a <strong>final walkthrough</strong> of the home before heading to the closing table. Confirm the home is in the agreed-upon condition, all seller-required repairs have been completed, and the seller has vacated.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Sign the documents, receive your keys, and celebrate. <strong>You're a homeowner.</strong></p>
