<p><em>Rob Miller, Branch Manager at MadCity Home Loans, shares what he tells Realtor partners about making their buyers the most competitive offer in the room.</em></p>

<h3>How do I write a stronger offer in a Wisconsin seller's market?</h3>

<p>
  As a Realtor, your negotiation skills are only as strong as the financial foundation behind your buyer. Here's what I tell every agent partner about stacking the mortgage side of a competitive offer:
</p>

<h3>1. Get a Full Underwrite — Not Just a Pre-Approval Letter</h3>
<p>
  A standard pre-approval letter means a loan officer reviewed the file. An <strong>underwritten pre-approval</strong> means an actual underwriter has reviewed income, assets, and credit and issued a conditional approval. This is a near-guarantee, and listing agents know the difference. We offer this at MadCity — ask us for it before your buyer writes an offer.
</p>

<h3>2. Call the Listing Agent</h3>
<p>
  I will personally call the listing agent before your offer is submitted to introduce the buyer, confirm our turnaround times, and answer any financing questions. A lender who picks up the phone is a massive confidence signal to the seller's side.
</p>

<h3>3. Short Financing Contingency Window</h3>
<p>
  Most offers come in with 21-day financing contingencies. When my file is already underwritten, we can often commit to <strong>14 days or fewer</strong>. This is a real, meaningful competitive advantage on a tight timeline.
</p>

<h3>4. Offer a Larger Earnest Money Deposit</h3>
<p>
  A higher EMD signals serious intent. Combined with an underwritten pre-approval, it tells the seller this deal is getting to the closing table.
</p>
