<p class="wp-block-paragraph">
  At <strong>MadCity Home Loans</strong>, our mission goes far beyond home financing. We love working closely with our community and strategic partners, making every event feel like catching up with friends and family. That is why Rob Miller and the MadCity Home Loans team are incredibly proud to participate in and attend the upcoming <strong>2026 Drive for Dreams Golf Outing</strong>, benefiting our 501(c)(3) nonprofit partner, <strong>Morgan's Caring Connection</strong>.
</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">
  Morgan's Caring Connection, led by Janis Henslee, provides invaluable support, advocacy, and preparedness resources to individuals with disabilities and their families. Through our partnership, we support the <strong>Family Support Giving Fund</strong>, helping families cover crucial legal costs like Special Needs Trusts, Guardianship, Wills, and Power of Attorney.
</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Event Details</h3>
<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li><strong>Event:</strong> Drive for Dreams Golf Outing</li>
  <li><strong>Date:</strong> Friday, June 26, 2026</li>
  <li><strong>Nonprofit Partner:</strong> Morgan's Caring Connection</li>
  <li><strong>Goal:</strong> Raising funds for special needs family support, legal resources, and community advocacy.</li>
</ul>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">
  We invite our friends, clients, and real estate partners to join us at this wonderful outing. It is always a fantastic opportunity to connect, catch up, and support an amazing cause together.
