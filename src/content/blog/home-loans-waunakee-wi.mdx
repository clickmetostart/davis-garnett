<h2>Loan Programs Rob Miller Offers Waunakee Buyers</h2>

<h3>Conventional Loans</h3>

<p>The workhorse of the Waunakee market. Conventional financing is available with as little as 3–5% down for qualifying buyers. The conforming loan limit for Wisconsin is currently $806,500, which covers most Waunakee purchases — including many in new construction subdivisions. For homes above that threshold, jumbo options are available.</p>

<h3>FHA Loans</h3>

<p>FHA financing is a strong option for first-time buyers or buyers with smaller down payments and credit scores in the mid-600s. The FHA loan limit for Dane County currently sits above $540,000, making it a viable choice for a significant portion of the Waunakee market.</p>

<p>Veterans, active-duty service members, and eligible surviving spouses purchasing in Waunakee can access VA financing with zero down payment and no private mortgage insurance. Rob Miller is an experienced VA lender who handles VA purchases throughout Dane County.</p>
