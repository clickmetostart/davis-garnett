<p><em>Rob Miller, Branch Manager at MadCity Home Loans, explains WHEDA — Wisconsin's most powerful first-time buyer tool that most buyers have never heard of.</em></p>

<h3>What is the WHEDA loan program and how do I qualify?</h3>

<p>
  WHEDA stands for the Wisconsin Housing and Economic Development Authority. It's a state agency that offers below-market interest rate mortgages and down payment assistance to income-qualified Wisconsin buyers — primarily first-time buyers or those buying in targeted areas.
</p>

<h3>WHEDA Advantage (The Core Program)</h3>
<p>
  This is a 30-year, fixed-rate conventional loan offered at rates that are typically <strong>0.25–0.75% below market</strong>. That's a meaningful savings over 30 years. It can be combined with WHEDA's down payment assistance programs.
</p>

<h3>Who Qualifies?</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>First-time buyer:</strong> You must not have owned a home in the past 3 years (waived in some targeted areas)</li>
  <li><strong>Income limits:</strong> Vary by county and household size. In Dane County, limits run approximately $91,000–$105,000 depending on household size</li>
  <li><strong>Purchase price limits:</strong> Also county-specific, but typically accommodate most starter and mid-range homes in Wisconsin</li>
  <li><strong>Credit:</strong> Minimum 620–640 depending on program</li>
  <li><strong>Primary residence only</strong></li>
</ul>

<h3>WHEDA Down Payment Assistance</h3>
<p>
  WHEDA offers an "Easy Close" DPA product — a 10-year second mortgage at a low rate (sometimes 0%) to cover your down payment and closing costs. When combined with the below-market first mortgage, this program can dramatically reduce your upfront cash need.
</p>
