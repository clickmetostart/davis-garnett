<h3>What is a jumbo loan and do I need one in Wisconsin?</h3>

<p>
  A jumbo loan is any mortgage that exceeds the conforming loan limit set by the Federal Housing Finance Agency (FHFA). In 2026, the conforming loan limit for most Wisconsin counties is <strong>$806,500</strong>. Any loan above that threshold is a jumbo loan.
</p>

<h3>What Makes Jumbo Loans Different?</h3>
<p>
  Because jumbo loans can't be sold to Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac, lenders hold them on their own books — which means stricter qualification requirements:
</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Credit score:</strong> Typically 700–720 minimum, with 740+ for the best rates</li>
  <li><strong>Down payment:</strong> Usually 10–20% down required; some lenders go as low as 5–10% for strong borrowers</li>
  <li><strong>Reserves:</strong> 6–12 months of PITI (principal, interest, taxes, insurance) reserves in liquid assets</li>
  <li><strong>DTI:</strong> Usually capped at 43–45%, stricter than conventional</li>
  <li><strong>Documentation:</strong> Thorough income documentation required — these loans get fully manually reviewed</li>
</ul>

<h3>Do I Need a Jumbo Loan in Wisconsin?</h3>
<p>
  In the Madison market, jumbo loans are most commonly needed for:
</p>
<ul>
  <li>High-end single-family homes in Nakoma, Maple Bluff, or Shorewood Hills</li>
  <li>Large lakefront properties on Mendota, Monona, or Waubesa</li>
  <li>Move-up buyers in western suburbs like Middleton or Waunakee purchasing $900K+ homes</li>
</ul>
