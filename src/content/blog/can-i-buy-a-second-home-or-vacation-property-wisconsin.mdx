<h3>Can I buy a second home or vacation property in Wisconsin?</h3>

<p>
  Yes — Wisconsin's lakes, Dells area, and Door County make second home purchases very common here. Here's what you need to know about the financing:
</p>

<h3>Second Home vs. Investment Property: The Key Distinction</h3>
<p>
  Lenders care deeply about how you intend to use the property. A <strong>second home</strong> is one you occupy personally for a portion of the year and don't rent out as a primary income source. An <strong>investment property</strong> is one you rent out consistently. The classification affects your rate and down payment significantly.
</p>

<h3>Second Home Requirements (Conventional)</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Down payment:</strong> Minimum 10% down</li>
  <li><strong>Credit score:</strong> 680+ recommended; 620 minimum</li>
  <li><strong>Rates:</strong> Typically 0.25–0.5% higher than primary residence rates</li>
  <li><strong>Occupancy:</strong> Must be a reasonable distance from your primary home OR be in a typical vacation area</li>
  <li><strong>Rental:</strong> Short-term rentals (Airbnb/VRBO) are generally allowed as long as personal use is primary</li>
</ul>

<h3>Investment Property Requirements</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Down payment:</strong> Minimum 15–25% down</li>
  <li><strong>Rates:</strong> 0.5–0.75% higher than primary residence</li>
  <li><strong>Rental income:</strong> Can be used to help qualify (75% of gross rent counted)</li>
</ul>

<h3>Can I Use VA or FHA for a Second Home?</h3>
<p>
  No. VA and FHA loans are strictly for primary residences. You must use conventional financing (or portfolio/DSCR loans) for second homes and investment properties.
</p>
