<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Rob Miller and the MadCity Home Loans Team are now listed as a featured business partner with Unions News, connecting union members across Wisconsin and beyond with local, accountable mortgage guidance.</p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What This Partnership Means</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Unions News serves as a resource hub for labor union members, covering union news and connecting members with vetted business partners in their communities. Rob Miller's inclusion as a business partner gives union members and their families direct access to a lending team that processes loans locally in Wisconsin — meaning faster decisions, direct communication, and dependable closings without the delays that come from out-of-state underwriting.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">For union members planning a home purchase, <a href="/knowledge-hub/refinancing-guide-rob-miller-provisor">refinance</a>, or simply trying to understand their options, this partnership offers a straightforward way to connect with a lender who treats every client's situation individually rather than running them through a one-size-fits-all process.</p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Why It Matters for Union Families</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Union members often have income structures — overtime, prevailing wage work, seasonal schedules, or multiple income sources — that don't always fit neatly into standard mortgage underwriting boxes. Rob's team has spent over 20 years working through exactly these kinds of situations, translating complex loan options into plain language and tailoring guidance to each client's actual financial picture and long-term goals.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This local-first, education-focused approach is the same philosophy that's guided Rob's work across Wisconsin, Illinois, Iowa, Minnesota, Michigan, Florida, Texas, North Dakota, South Dakota, Colorado, and Washington.</p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Connecting Through Unions News</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Union members can find Rob Miller's listing directly through the Unions News Business Partner Portal, where his team's contact information, service area, and background are available alongside other vetted partners serving the labor community.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Whether you're a union member exploring homeownership for the first time or looking to refinance, this new connection makes it easier to reach a lending team that understands your work and your goals.</p>
