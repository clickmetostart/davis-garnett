<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Every year, hundreds of talented professionals relocate to Madison, Wisconsin to join the University of Wisconsin-Madison and UW Health. Whether you are incoming faculty, a medical resident, or administrative staff, moving to a new city brings a unique set of challenges—especially when trying to navigate a highly competitive housing market.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Rob Miller and the MadCity Home Loans team have spent decades helping UW professionals plant roots in Dane County. This guide is designed to help you understand the Madison real estate landscape, choose the right neighborhood for your commute, and secure the best financing for your situation.</p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">1. The Commute: Where to Live Near Campus</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The UW-Madison campus is nestled between Lake Mendota and Lake Monona, right in the heart of the city. Because the campus is centrally located on an isthmus, traffic and parking can be significant factors in your daily routine. Here are some of the most popular neighborhoods for UW faculty and staff:</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Shorewood Hills & Village of Shorewood</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Located just west of campus, Shorewood Hills is an independent village surrounded by Madison. It is highly sought after by established faculty and medical professionals due to its beautiful historic homes, excellent public schools, and incredibly short commute to UW Hospital and the main campus. Homes here command a premium, but the investment is historically very stable.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">The Near West Side (Monroe Street, Vilas, Dudgeon-Monroe)</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If you want a walkable, vibrant neighborhood with coffee shops, local restaurants, and quick access to campus via bike or bus, the Near West Side is ideal. The Monroe Street corridor and the area surrounding Henry Vilas Zoo offer charming early-20th-century homes. The proximity to Camp Randall Stadium means you'll be in the heart of Badger game day energy.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Hilldale & Midvale Heights</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Slightly further west, these neighborhoods offer a mix of mid-century ranches and modern condo developments. The Hilldale Shopping Center provides upscale dining, shopping, and groceries (including Whole Foods). The commute down University Avenue to campus is straightforward and well-serviced by Madison Metro Transit.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Middleton & Fitchburg</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If you prefer more space, larger yards, or newer construction, the surrounding suburbs of Middleton (to the west) and Fitchburg (to the south) are excellent choices. Both offer fantastic school districts and parks. The commute to campus is typically 15-25 minutes, depending on traffic.</p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">2. Condos vs. Single-Family Homes</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Many incoming faculty and medical residents debate between buying a condo or a single-family home. Both are excellent options in Madison, but they serve different needs.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Why Choose a Condo?</h3>
<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li><strong>Low Maintenance:</strong> Wisconsin winters mean snow shoveling. If you have a demanding academic or clinical schedule, a condo association handles snow removal, lawn care, and exterior maintenance.</li>
  <li><strong>Location:</strong> Condos are often located closer to the downtown core and campus (e.g., Capitol Square, the Isthmus, or Hilldale).</li>
  <li><strong>Affordability:</strong> For early-career professionals or residents, condos often provide a more accessible entry point into the Madison market while still building equity.</li>
</ul>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>Read our complete <a href="/knowledge-hub/madison-condo-buying-refinancing-guide">Madison Condo Buying Guide</a> for more details on navigating condo associations and financing.</em></p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Why Choose a Single-Family Home?</h3>
<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li><strong>Space & Privacy:</strong> Ideal if you have a growing family, pets, or need dedicated home office space for research and grading.</li>
  <li><strong>Appreciation:</strong> Single-family homes in desirable Madison neighborhoods historically appreciate at a very strong rate.</li>
  <li><strong>No HOA Fees:</strong> While you are responsible for maintenance, you aren't subject to monthly association dues or special assessments.</li>
</ul>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">3. Timing Your Move and Financing</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Academic hiring cycles often dictate when you need to move. If you are starting a position in the fall semester, you ideally want to close on a home in June or July. This means you should begin the pre-approval and house-hunting process in <strong>March or April</strong>.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Using Your Offer Letter for Financing</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">One of the biggest concerns for relocating professionals is proving income before they have started their new job. As a UW-Madison or UW Health employee, you do not necessarily need a month of pay stubs to get a mortgage.</p>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Rob Miller’s team can often use your <strong>signed offer letter or employment contract</strong> to qualify you for a loan, provided your start date is within a specific window (usually 60-90 days) of closing. This allows you to close on your new home and get settled <em>before</em> your first day on campus.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Understanding Dane County Property Taxes</h3>
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">It is important to know that property taxes in Dane County are generally higher than the national average. This is what funds our excellent public schools, parks, and city services. When you get pre-approved with MadCity Home Loans, we run exact calculations that include estimated property taxes, ensuring your monthly payment (PITI) is accurate and comfortable.</p>
